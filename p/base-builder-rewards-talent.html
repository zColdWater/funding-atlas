Base Builder Rewards / Top Base Builders (Talent)

Base, operated by Talent (Talent Protocol) · automatic monthly rewards by activity

Unconfirmedchecked 2026-11-20

Is it still open?

As of 2026-11-20: Unconfirmed — Program page or terms exist, but we found no 2026 evidence it is still taking part or paying out.

Amount
Latest terms we could read (last updated 2025-12-29): 5 ETH for every 500 builders each month, split across 4 tiers
Payout
ETH
Who
Builders with prior activity connected on talent.app (or Talent Plus), a Basename and an ETH address; sanctions screening applies
How
No application: rewards were distributed automatically based on GitHub, Base contract and Mini App activity
Deadline
Terms set no end date. Talent's acquirer announced the end of Talent's commercial operations, with its API, Talent+ and social channels shutting down at the end of August 2026
Official link
web.archive.org/web/20260317155412/https://docs.talentprotocol.com/docs/legal/builder-rewards-terms-conditions

Notes

docs.talentprotocol.com no longer resolves (checked 2026-11-20), so we cite the 2026-03-17 web.archive.org snapshot of the terms, which by then described the monthly 'Top Base Builders' program. RootData (2026-08-20) reports Talent Protocol was acquired by Interplanetary Talent Services and that over $600,000 had been paid through Builder Rewards, but says nothing about the program's future. We found no 2026 statement from Base or Talent that it continues. Treat any 'Base pays builders weekly/monthly' claim as unverified.
